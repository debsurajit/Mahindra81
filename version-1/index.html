01 / PURPOSE

A LEGACY THAT KEEPS RISING.

For 81 years, Mahindra has believed that progress is meaningful when it creates opportunities for everyone.

What began in 1945 as a bold enterprise has grown into a global group working across multiple industries and markets.

02 / OUR STORY

A JOURNEY OF RISE.

Every milestone added another chapter to the Mahindra story.

1945

The Beginning

Mahindra & Mohammed was founded on October 2, 1945 by J.C. Mahindra, K.C. Mahindra and Ghulam Mohammed.

1947

The Jeep Arrives

The iconic Jeep arrived in India, marking an important chapter in Mahindra's automotive journey.

1956

A New Chapter

Mahindra became a publicly listed company, opening a new chapter of growth and enterprise.

1961

Expanding Horizons

Mahindra entered a new phase through its collaboration with International Harvester.

2020

The Thar Rises

The new Thar represented another expression of Mahindra's spirit of adventure and capability.

2022

New Icons

The Scorpio-N added another milestone to Mahindra's continuing automotive story.

2026

The Rise Continues

81 years later, the journey continues with a focus on creating positive change and building the future.
